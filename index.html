1. Overview

Raju's Studio ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to respecting and protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the information collected through Arrow Maze: Logic Escape (the "Game") distributed via the Google Play Store.

Arrow Maze: Logic Escape is a casual, offline-friendly logic puzzle game designed to offer relaxing mindflow and puzzle solving without intrusive data collection or third-party ad tracking.

2. Information We Collect

A. Sign-in Information (Optional)

If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we may receive your public display name, email address, and profile photo URL to synchronize your game progress across multiple devices and show your position on global leaderboards. You can play completely anonymously as a guest without signing in.

B. Game Progress & Local Storage

Your unlocked levels, high scores, collected stars, selected visual themes, and local audio preferences are stored locally on your device via standard Android sandboxed storage.

C. Device & Performance Diagnostics

Basic non-identifying technical data such as operating system version, device model, and crash logs may be processed via Google Play Services to ensure compatibility, bug fixes, and stability.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect your privacy. We do NOT:

  • Request or access your physical contacts or address book.
  • Access your microphone, camera, or device photo gallery.
  • Track precise real-time GPS location.
  • Collect financial, banking, or credit card information.
  • Sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game utilizes trusted, industry-standard Google services to deliver reliable gameplay:

  • Google Play Services: For app updates, cloud save, and secure distribution. (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase (Optional): For cloud progress synchronization and competitive leaderboard services. (Privacy Policy)

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Arrow Maze: Logic Escape is suitable for general audiences of all ages, including children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete it.

6. Data Security

We implement standard industry-recommended administrative and technical safeguards to protect your game data. All cloud transmissions (if enabled) are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols.

7. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion

Local Data: You can delete all locally stored game progress at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings > Apps > Arrow Maze > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.

Cloud Account Data Deletion: If you signed in and wish to permanently delete your associated account, leaderboard entries, or cloud saves, you can request full deletion by emailing us at rajvadaviya1@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Arrow Maze". We will process and permanently remove your records within 7 business days.
