## 1. Overview
Raju's Studio ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile application **Arrow Maze: Logic Escape** (the "Game") distributed via the Google Play Store.

Arrow Maze: Logic Escape is a casual, offline-friendly logic puzzle game designed to offer relaxing mindflow and puzzle solving without intrusive data collection or third-party ad tracking.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect minimal and strictly necessary information required to operate and improve gameplay features:

### A. Information You Provide Directly
- **Sign-in Information (Optional):** If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we may receive your public display name, email address, and profile photo URL to sync your game progress across devices and display your name on optional leaderboards. You can play completely anonymously as a guest without signing in.

### B. Game Progress & Local Storage
- **Offline Save Data:** Completed level milestones, high scores, collected stars, selected visual themes, and local sound settings are stored locally on your device storage via standard secure Android sandboxed storage.

### C. Automatically Collected Technical Data
- **Device & Performance Diagnostics:** Basic non-identifying technical data such as operating system version, device model, language preference, and crash logs may be automatically processed via Google Play Services to ensure compatibility, bug fixes, and stability.

---

## 3. Information We Do NOT Collect
We respect your personal privacy. We do **NOT**:
- Request or collect your physical contacts or address book.
- Access your microphone, camera, or device photo gallery.
- Track precise real-time GPS location.
- Collect financial, banking, or credit card information.
- Sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or brokers.

## 7. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion
- **Local Data:** You can delete all locally stored game progress at any time by clearing the application data in your Android **Settings > Apps > Arrow Maze > Storage > Clear Data**, or by uninstalling the application.
- **Cloud Account Data Deletion:** If you signed in and wish to permanently delete your associated account, leaderboard entries, or cloud saves, you can request full deletion by emailing us at:  
  **`rajvadaviya1@gmail.com`** with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request - Arrow Maze"*. We will process and permanently remove your records within 7 business days.
